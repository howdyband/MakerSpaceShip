Safety Protocols & Maker Merit Badges

A collaborative shop is a safe shop.

Because we integrate high school students, supervise adults with developmental disabilities, and community makers under one roof, safety is our ultimate priority. We actively discourage "Lone Rangers" who bypass community interaction. Accountability is built into our layout and culture.

The Badge System

Every member receives a personalized cubby at the entrance for safety glasses, ear protection, and gloves. You are also issued a clip-on ID badge that must be worn at all times.

  • Tool Competency Stickers: Your badge features a grid system. As you demonstrate safe use of heavy machinery, a Shop/Studio Captain awards you a color-coded sticker for that tool.
  • Visual Enforcement: If you are seen operating a power tool without the correct sticker on your name tag, the tool will be immediately powered down.

Earning "Merit Badges"

Beyond basic safety, we celebrate mastery. Members earn honorary badges for demonstrating advanced competencies:

  • The Rigger: Demonstrate 10 functional, load-bearing knots.
  • The Musician: Demonstrate a scale on a fretted, unfretted, woodwind, and brass instrument.
  • The Jig Master: Design and build a reusable, safe jig for our woodshop.
  • The Seamstress: Sew on a button, darn a sock, and demo our CNC embrodery machine.

The Sponsorship Requirement & Paying it Forward

To upgrade to the $90/month 24/7 access tier, a member cannot simply pay their way in. They must log 20 hours of shop time and be "sponsored" by an existing Advanced Maker. Furthermore, to earn a new machine competency sticker, you must first assist someone else using it.
