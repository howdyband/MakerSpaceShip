The "Stackable" Incentive Discounts

We heavily discount memberships in exchange for your expertise and commitment to our youth. Stack these $20 monthly discounts to lower your dues by up to $60 per month:

  • Mentor Discount (-$20/mo): Pass a Siuslaw School District background check to work with LearnSTEAM youth.
  • Safety Discount (-$20/mo): Provide proof of current CPR/First Aid and OSHA-10 certification.
  • Trade Expert Discount (-$20/mo): Hold an active, professional certification or journey-level card relevant to our shops (e.g., CCB Licensed Contractor, AWS Certified Welder, ASE Automotive Technician, FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot, HAM Radio Operator, NIMS Machining Certification, Journeyman Electrician, IPC Soldering Certification, or OSHA 30).
  • Host/Captain Discount (-$20/mo): Commit to 10 hours a month monitoring shop floor safety or hosting our retail storefront.
  • Gain Safety Certifications (-$20/mo): Demonstrate competent/safe operation of all tools in the work shop, tech studio, or music studio.

Consumables & The Honor Box

We utilize a transparent plexiglass Honor Box for cost of materials:

  • Wood: Calculate board feet and pay per species.
